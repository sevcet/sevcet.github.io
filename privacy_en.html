1. INTRODUCTION
This Privacy Policy explains how MyVideoBox ("we", "us", "App") collects, uses, and shares your personal data when you use our App.

2. DATA WE COLLECT
2.1. Data you enter:
     - Catalog and M3U list URLs
     - Content of catalogs you add
     - App settings (theme, language)
     - Watch history and favorites (stored locally on device)

2.2. Data we automatically collect:
     - Device type and operating system
     - App version
     - Device language
     - Error data (crash logs)

2.3. Data collected by third parties:
     - Google AdMob (advertising ID, IP address, device data)
     - Google Play Billing (transaction data)

3. HOW WE USE DATA
3.1. To provide the video playback service.
3.2. To store your settings and history.
3.3. To display personalized ads (with your consent).
3.4. To analyze errors and improve the App.
3.5. To process subscriptions through Google Play.

4. DATA SHARING
4.1. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
4.2. We share data with:
     - Google (AdMob, Play Billing) — for ads and payments
     - Google AdMob network partners (only with your consent)

5. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)
If you are located in the EU, you have the following rights:
5.1. Right to access your data.
5.2. Right to correct inaccurate data.
5.3. Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten").
5.4. Right to restrict processing.
5.5. Right to data portability.
5.6. Right to object to processing.
5.7. Right to withdraw consent at any time.

6. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES
6.1. The App uses cookies and similar technologies for:
     - Remembering your settings
     - Ad personalization (with consent)
     - Analytics

7. DATA SECURITY
7.1. We take reasonable measures to protect your data.
7.2. All data transmitted over the internet is protected by HTTPS protocol.
7.3. Local data is stored in the App's private storage.

8. CHILDREN
8.1. The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age.
8.2. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
