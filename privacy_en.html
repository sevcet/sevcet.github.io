1. INTRODUCTION
This Privacy Policy explains how MyVideoBox ("we", "us", "App") handles data when you use our App. MyVideoBox is designed to respect your privacy and to not collect personal data from users.

2. DATA AND LOCAL STORAGE

2.1. Locally stored data (not collected, does not leave the device):

     The App stores the following data EXCLUSIVELY locally on your device:

     - URLs of catalogs and M3U lists that you have entered
     - Content of catalogs that you have added
     - App settings (theme, language)
     - Watch history and favorites
     - Legally required acceptance (legal acceptance)

     This data is NOT transmitted to our servers, is NOT shared with
     third parties, and is available only to you. It is removed by
     uninstalling the App or by clearing App data in your device settings.

     MyVideoBox does not have a server that would collect, process, or
     store your data. The App is fully self-contained and operates
     without any communication with our servers.

2.2. Data collected by third parties (Google):

     The App uses services provided by Google that collect certain data
     in accordance with Google's privacy policies:

     - Google AdMob — advertising ID, IP address, device data, type and
       version of the operating system
     - Google Play Billing — subscription transaction data
     - Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) — consent data

     MyVideoBox does not have access to this data and does not process it.
     For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy:
     https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. HOW DATA IS USED

3.1. Local data (section 2.1) is used exclusively for the functioning
     of the App itself on your device:
     - to play videos from catalogs you have added
     - to store your settings and watch history
     - to display your favorite movies and series

3.2. Data collected by Google (section 2.2) is used for:
     - displaying ads in the free version of the App
     - processing subscriptions through the Google Play platform
     - managing consent (GDPR/UMP)

4. DATA SHARING

4.1. MyVideoBox does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with
     third parties, because it does not collect it.

4.2. Data collected by Google is shared in accordance with Google's
     privacy policies and Google AdMob network policies.

5. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, you have the following rights regarding
data collected by Google:

5.1. Right to access your data.
5.2. Right to correct inaccurate data.
5.3. Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten").
5.4. Right to restrict processing.
5.5. Right to data portability.
5.6. Right to object to processing.
5.7. Right to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights regarding Google data, please contact Google
directly. For local data on your device, you can delete it at any time
by clearing the App data.

6. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

6.1. The App uses local storage (SharedPreferences) to remember your
     settings and catalog data.

6.2. The WebView component used for YouTube playback may use cookies
     in accordance with Google and YouTube policies.

7. DATA SECURITY

7.1. All local data is stored in the App's private storage, to which
     other applications do not have access.

7.2. All data transmitted over the internet is protected by the HTTPS
     protocol.

7.3. MyVideoBox does not have a server or database, which eliminates
     the risk of data leakage on our side.

8. CHILDREN AND MINOR USERS

8.1. The App is not intended for persons under 18 years of age.

8.2. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18 years of age.

8.3. If we learn that a person under 18 years of age has used the App,
     we will take steps to remove all local data and will notify the
     authorities if necessary.

8.4. If you are a parent or guardian and learn that your child under 18
     years of age has used the App, please contact us so that appropriate
     measures can be taken.

9. CHANGES TO POLICY

9.1. We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy.

9.2. We will notify you of significant changes via the App.
