How to delete your data - MyVideoBox

App name: MyVideoBox
Developer: Sait Salimovic
Package: com.myvideobox.player

Quick answer: MyVideoBox does not create any user accounts and does not store any personal data on our servers. All data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by following the steps below.

What data does MyVideoBox store?

  • Catalogs you added (JSON files, M3U lists) — stored locally on your device
  • Favorites — stored locally on your device
  • Watch history — stored locally on your device
  • App settings (theme, language) — stored locally on your device
  • Purchase information — managed by Google Play, not by MyVideoBox

How to delete all data

Option 1: Delete data through Android Settings (recommended)

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Apps (or Applications)
  3. Find and tap MyVideoBox
  4. Tap Storage & cache
  5. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data)

This will permanently delete all catalogs, favorites, watch history, and app settings.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

  1. Long-press the MyVideoBox icon on your home screen
  2. Tap Uninstall

Uninstalling removes all locally stored data.

Option 3: Delete specific data inside the app

  • Delete a catalog: Tap the − (minus) icon in the toolbar, select catalogs, confirm
  • Clear watch history: Open History from the menu, tap the trash icon
  • Remove favorites: Open any movie detail, tap the heart icon to unfavorite

What about my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely by Google Play, not by MyVideoBox. To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open Google Play subscriptions
  2. Find MyVideoBox
  3. Tap Cancel subscription

Data we keep

MyVideoBox does not keep any data after you delete it. Google Play may retain purchase records for legal and accounting purposes, according to their own policies.
